XPRESSABLE FUND · IN THE WORKS

No Family Should Miss Out Over Cost.

We're exploring starting XpressAble Fund, a nonprofit initiative to help cover the subscription cost for families who can't afford it — funded by people who want to help make sure this tool reaches every child who needs it.

Where this stands today: XpressAble Fund is not yet a registered nonprofit — we're gauging interest before taking on the cost and ongoing work of setting one up properly. Nothing here is tax-deductible yet, and no payments are processed on this page. If you apply or offer to help, we'll follow up directly by email.

Why a Fund

XpressAble is free to download, with a small subscription — $2.99/month or $24.99/year — to unlock full access. That price is set to cover the real, ongoing cost of the AI processing behind every conversation, not to turn a profit. But for some families, even a nominal subscription is a real barrier.

XpressAble Fund is meant to close that gap: people who want to support the mission can help cover the cost, and families who need it can apply for free access, no questions asked about why.

How It Would Work

Once the Fund is set up, here's the plan.

🙋

Families Apply

A short, private request — no proof of income or lengthy paperwork. If cost is the reason you or your child can't use XpressAble, that's reason enough to ask.

✅

We Grant Free Access

Approved families get full access to XpressAble at no cost — no card required, no trial that expires.

💙

Supporters Help Cover It

Contributions from people who believe in the mission fund those free accounts, so cost never has to be the reason a child goes without.
