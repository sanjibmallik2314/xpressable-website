What Is XpressAblePro?

XpressAblePro is a professional version of XpressAble, built specifically for speech-language pathologists and therapists who work with many clients at once. Instead of one profile on one family's phone, XpressAblePro gives you a single dashboard to set up, personalize, and maintain communication profiles across your entire caseload — each one feeding the same trusted AI engine that already listens, understands, and gently nudges a response for children with autism, speech delays, and other communication challenges.

It's the same core experience families know from XpressAble — listen to a question, generate a warm first-person response, speak it aloud through the child's headphones — now built for the reality of clinical practice, where you're supporting dozens of clients, not just one.

Still a nudge, not a crutch — at scale. Every client profile you build in XpressAblePro drives the same real-time AI prompting XpressAble is known for. You bring the clinical detail from your intake interview; the AI brain behind the conversation uses it to shape a response that actually sounds like that client.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is XpressAblePro available now?

Yes — XpressAblePro is live on the App Store. Download it here or scan the QR code above.

How is XpressAblePro different from XpressAble?

XpressAble is the free, consumer version families use to set up one profile for their own child. XpressAblePro is the professional version, built for therapists managing up to 50 client profiles from a single dashboard — powered by the same AI response engine.

What is the Caregiver Interview Worksheet for?

It mirrors the profile setup screen inside the app — name, age, interests, favorite things, special skills, topics to avoid, communication style, and preferred language — so you can run a structured intake interview with a caregiver and turn it directly into a client profile.

How many client profiles can I manage?

Up to 50 per professional account, each with its own personalized settings, preferred language, chat history, and trigger words.

What is "the AI brain" actually doing?

It reads the question that was asked, the client's profile details, and their communication style, then generates a short, natural, first-person response — a nudge toward what the client might want to say, not a scripted line.

What does "repeat through their headphones" mean?

Once a response is generated and spoken aloud, the client (or you) can tap to replay it as many times as needed through their own headphones — useful if they missed it the first time or want to hear it again before responding.

What devices does it run on?

XpressAblePro runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS), with headphone delivery — including bone-conduction headphones like the Shokz OpenRun.

Can I recommend the free XpressAble app to families as well?

Yes. XpressAble is live on the App Store today and free to download — a great complement for individual families alongside your use of XpressAblePro for your caseload.
