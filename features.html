Every Feature, in Depth

Everything you need to know to get the most out of XpressAble.

🎙️

Instant Speech Recognition

XpressAble uses OpenAI Whisper — one of the most accurate speech recognition systems available — to transcribe questions in real time. It handles accents, background noise, and natural speech patterns remarkably well.

You can set the listening duration to 5, 10 or 15 seconds depending on how long questions typically are in your child's environment. XpressAble also lets you stop early by tapping the button again.

Fast transcription Works in noisy rooms Accent friendly
🤖

Two Response Modes

Short Response keeps it to one sentence — ideal for quick back-and-forth conversations, classroom settings, or children who prefer minimal output. It uses a "sentence starter" technique for personal questions: instead of guessing, it gives the child a prompt like "I am feeling…?" which they can complete themselves.

Long Response gives a warmer two-sentence reply — answering the question first, then adding something natural like "How are you?" or a personal interest connection. Great for social conversations where a fuller answer feels more natural.

Short — 1 sentence Long — 2 sentences Sentence starters
🎧

Spoken Aloud Through Headphones

Every response is spoken clearly through the child's headphones — only they hear it. This creates a private, discreet experience that doesn't draw attention or create awkwardness in social settings.

XpressAble works especially well with bone-conduction headphones like Shokz OpenRun, which sit outside the ears and allow the child to remain fully aware of their surroundings while still hearing the response clearly.

The 🔊 Replay button lets the child hear the response again at any time without re-recording.

Private audio Shokz OpenRun compatible Replay anytime
👤

Personalised Child Profile

The Profile screen lets you enter your child's name, age, interests, favourite things, special skills, topics to avoid, and communication style preferences. This information is stored securely on your device only and never shared or transmitted.

The AI uses every detail to personalise responses. A child who loves music might get a response like "I am feeling great — I was thinking about music all morning!" rather than a generic answer.

You can choose between Short & Simple or Warm & Chatty response styles, and set an attention span level (short, medium, long) to control how complex the language is.

Stored on device only Personalised responses Adjustable style
🌍

Multi-Language Support

XpressAble supports English, Spanish, French and Hindi — with more languages planned. Both the incoming question and the outgoing response are handled in the selected language.

Switch languages instantly by tapping the language pill at the top of the screen. Perfect for multilingual families, bilingual classrooms, or children learning a second language.

English Español Français हिंदी
📋

Chat History & Feedback Loop

Every exchange is saved to Chat History — you can review all past questions and responses at any time. This is useful for spotting patterns, sharing with therapists, or just reflecting on your child's conversations.

For each exchange you can rate it 👍 Good or 👎 Needs work, add a note, and suggest a better response. This feedback is used directly to improve how XpressAble responds to your child over time — creating a personalised feedback loop that gets smarter with every conversation.
